Sixteen patent families on targeted or organ-selective lipid nanoparticles, including Bristol Myers Squibb and allied companies (Juno, Nitto Denko) and Orna, compared by target cell, cargo, disease, ionizable lipid chemistry and formulation. Click a row for claim 1, family members and the ionizable lipid used in each formulation.

How this was checked. The full text of the lead patents in families A to J was downloaded and read for claim 1, the examples and the formulation tables. For the BMS and allied families (K to P) I read the claims and the formulation text but not every example, and Orna (N) covers 13 of about 175 documents. Orbital Therapeutics' targeted-LNP filing for OTX-201 is not in the snapshot, so it is not in this table. Family members come from SureChEMBL (release 2026-08-18) and Google Patents. Ionizable-lipid structures are drawings in the patents, so they appear here by name or code only. In the three WO publications, some example tables are images, so those sizes and N/P ratios are marked "not stated". Not a legal or freedom-to-operate opinion.
