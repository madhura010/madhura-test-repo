# Literature Review: AI/ML Models for Predicting LNP Encapsulation Efficiency (EE%)

## 1. Executive Summary
Predictive modeling of Lipid Nanoparticle (LNP) encapsulation efficiency has transitioned from trial-and-error experimental screening to **physics-informed data science**. Current research focuses on three primary architectures: gradient-boosted ensemble learners, neural architectures embedding physical assembly constraints, and hybrid models incorporating Molecular Dynamics (MD) descriptors.

## 2. Technical Approaches

### A. Gradient Boosting and Ensemble Methods (e.g., XGBoost, LightGBM)
These models are currently the industry benchmark for modeling non-linear dependencies between formulation parameters (lipid stoichiometry, process variables) and output metrics (EE%). 
* **Key Finding:** **Self-Validated Ensemble Models (SVEM)** are reported to provide superior robustness, particularly when training on smaller, heterogeneous experimental datasets (Bhatia et al., 2023).

### B. Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs)
To mitigate data scarcity, PINNs embed the thermodynamic principles of LNP assembly into the model’s loss function, reducing the volume of labeled training data required to achieve high predictive accuracy (Kim et al., 2022).

### C. Hybrid Molecular Dynamics (MD)-ML Frameworks
A significant trend in recent literature is the development of hybrid models that integrate **Molecular Dynamics (MD)** simulations. These models use MD to generate high-fidelity structural descriptors, such as particle morphology, lipid-RNA interaction energy, and internal LNP structural stability, which are then used as inputs to ML models to predict encapsulation outcomes (Gomez & Zhang, 2024).

## 3. Critical Challenges
* **Data Heterogeneity:** Lack of standardization in assay protocols prevents generalized, cross-lab model validation (Patel et al., 2024).
* **Dataset Scarcity:** Reliance on private, non-reproducible datasets. Open-access initiatives like the *LNP Atlas* are crucial for future progress (Wang et al., 2023).
